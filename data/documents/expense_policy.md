# Expense Policy

## Business Expense Rules

Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable and necessary business expenses
that are directly related to approved company work. Expenses must be ordinary,
cost-conscious, properly documented, and compliant with local tax rules.

Eligible expenses may include approved business travel, customer meeting costs,
training fees, professional membership fees, required office supplies, and
project-related materials. Personal expenses, fines, entertainment without
business purpose, luxury upgrades, and expenses for family members are not
reimbursable.

Employees should use company-preferred vendors and negotiated rates whenever
available. Managers are responsible for reviewing whether expenses are
reasonable, budgeted, and connected to legitimate business activity.

## Approval Flow

Expense approval follows the employee's reporting line. Expenses below 300 USD
or local equivalent require direct manager approval. Expenses from 300 USD to
2,000 USD require direct manager and department head approval. Expenses above
2,000 USD require Finance review in addition to department head approval.

Pre-approval is required for travel, conference registration, customer events,
software subscriptions, equipment purchases, and any expense expected to exceed
300 USD. Employees should request approval before committing company funds.

Finance may reject or return an expense report if documentation is incomplete,
the business purpose is unclear, the expense violates policy, or the approval
chain is missing.

## Travel Expense Policy

Business travel must be approved before booking. Employees should choose
cost-effective routes, standard economy airfare, and reasonably priced hotels
near the business location. Premium cabins, luxury hotels, and route changes for
personal convenience are not reimbursable unless approved as an exception.

Meals during business travel may be reimbursed up to the local daily limit set
by Finance. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless it is part of an approved customer
event. Laundry, minibar charges, in-room entertainment, and personal sightseeing
costs are not reimbursable.

Employees may combine personal travel with business travel only when the
business cost does not increase and the arrangement is approved in advance.
Additional costs caused by personal travel must be paid by the employee.

## Receipt Requirements

Receipts are required for all expenses above 25 USD or local equivalent. A valid
receipt must show the vendor name, transaction date, items or services
purchased, amount paid, currency, and payment method. Credit card statements
alone are not sufficient unless Finance grants an exception.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction date.
Reports submitted after 60 days may require department head approval and may be
rejected if the delay prevents proper accounting or tax treatment.

If a receipt is lost, the employee must provide a written explanation, business
purpose, transaction amount, date, vendor, and manager confirmation. Repeated
lost receipt claims may be reviewed by Finance and HR.
