# Company Policy

## Company Overview

Acme Operations Group is a regional enterprise services company that provides
business process support, internal technology operations, and customer success
services for corporate clients. The company operates with a focus on reliability,
professional conduct, data protection, and continuous improvement.

Employees are expected to act with integrity, respect colleagues and customers,
protect company information, and follow approved procedures when making business
decisions. Managers are responsible for communicating policy changes to their
teams and ensuring that employees understand the standards that apply to their
roles.

## Working Hours

The standard working schedule is Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with
a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
Employees should be available for meetings, customer escalations, and internal
coordination during core collaboration hours unless they are on approved leave.

Hourly employees must record their working time accurately in the approved time
tracking system. Salaried employees are expected to manage their time responsibly
and inform their manager if workload, deadlines, or personal circumstances affect
their availability.

Overtime must be approved by a manager in advance. Emergency overtime may be
approved after the fact only when required to protect customer operations, system
availability, or business continuity.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week when their role, customer
requirements, and team schedule allow it. Remote work must be approved by the
employee's direct manager. Some positions, such as facilities support, secure
document handling, and on-site customer operations, may require regular office
attendance.

Remote employees must maintain a safe and professional work environment, use
company-approved devices, and connect through approved security controls such as
VPN and multi-factor authentication. Company data must not be downloaded to
personal devices or shared through unapproved cloud storage services.

Managers may require employees to attend the office for team planning, training,
customer meetings, performance discussions, audits, or operational incidents.
Employees should provide at least one business day of notice if they need to
change an approved remote work day.

## Leave Policy

Employees accrue paid annual leave according to their employment contract and
local labor regulations. Leave requests should be submitted through the HR system
at least five business days before the requested start date. Managers approve
leave based on staffing levels, project deadlines, customer commitments, and
fairness across the team.

Sick leave should be reported to the manager as soon as possible, preferably
before the start of the workday. Medical documentation may be requested for sick
leave lasting three or more consecutive working days, or where required by local
law.

Emergency leave may be granted for urgent family, medical, or personal matters.
Employees should contact their manager as soon as practical and update the HR
system when they are able to do so.

Unused annual leave may be carried over only when allowed by local policy.
Managers should encourage employees to take leave regularly to support health,
sustainable work habits, and long-term performance.

## Public Holiday Policy

The company observes the official public holidays of the employee's assigned
work location. HR publishes the annual public holiday calendar before the start
of each calendar year. Employees working across countries should follow the
holiday calendar assigned in the HR system.

If a public holiday falls on a weekend, substitute holidays are handled according
to local law and HR guidance. Employees required to work on a public holiday due
to customer operations, system maintenance, or emergency support may receive
substitute time off or additional pay where required by law or contract.

Managers must plan staffing in advance for teams that provide holiday coverage.
Holiday coverage assignments should be communicated at least two weeks before
the holiday whenever possible.
