# HR Policy

## Employee Onboarding

New employees complete a structured onboarding program during their first 30
days. HR coordinates employment documentation, payroll setup, benefits
enrollment, and policy acknowledgement. The hiring manager is responsible for
role-specific training, team introductions, system access requests, and the first
month work plan.

On the first working day, employees receive access to the HR system, company
email, collaboration tools, and required security training. Access to sensitive
systems is granted only after manager approval and completion of mandatory
security training.

During the first week, managers should review job expectations, working hours,
communication channels, probation criteria, and escalation paths. During the
first 30 days, managers should hold at least two check-in meetings to review
progress, clarify responsibilities, and identify training needs.

## Salary Information Policy

Salary information is confidential personal data. HR and authorized managers may
access salary records only for legitimate business purposes such as payroll,
compensation planning, promotion review, compliance reporting, and approved
budget forecasting.

Employees must not disclose another employee's salary, bonus, allowance, or
payroll record unless they are explicitly authorized to handle that information.
Salary files must be stored only in approved HR systems or secured finance
repositories. Salary spreadsheets must not be sent through personal email or
stored in public shared drives.

Employees may discuss their own compensation with HR or their manager. Salary
adjustment requests are reviewed during the annual compensation cycle, promotion
reviews, or exceptional retention cases approved by HR and the department head.

## Performance Review Process

Formal performance reviews are conducted twice per year. The mid-year review
focuses on progress, feedback, goal adjustment, and development needs. The
year-end review evaluates results, role expectations, behavioral standards, and
future growth opportunities.

Employees complete a self-assessment before the review meeting. Managers provide
written feedback based on goals, work quality, collaboration, customer impact,
ownership, and adherence to company policies. Feedback should be specific,
evidence-based, and connected to role expectations.

Performance ratings are calibrated by department leadership and HR to support
consistency across teams. A low performance rating may result in a performance
improvement plan with clear expectations, support actions, review dates, and
measurable outcomes.

## Promotion Rules

Promotion decisions are based on sustained performance at the next level,
business need, role scope, leadership behavior, and demonstrated capability.
Employees are not promoted based only on tenure or completion of routine duties.

A manager may nominate an employee for promotion during the official promotion
cycle. The nomination should include evidence of expanded responsibilities,
impact, skill growth, stakeholder feedback, and readiness for the target role.
HR reviews promotion proposals for fairness, compensation alignment, and policy
compliance.

Promotions outside the standard cycle require approval from the department head
and HR. Exceptions are reserved for role changes, retention risks, organizational
restructuring, or urgent business needs.

## Training Policy

Employees must complete mandatory training assigned by HR, Legal, IT Security,
or department leadership. Mandatory training may include information security,
workplace conduct, data protection, anti-harassment, compliance, and role-based
operational procedures.

Job-related professional training may be approved when it supports current role
performance or planned career development. Employees should discuss training
requests with their manager before registration. Requests may be approved based
on budget, relevance, timing, and expected business benefit.

Employees who attend company-funded training are expected to share relevant
learnings with their team when appropriate. Certification costs may be reimbursed
only when pre-approved and supported by receipts or official completion records.
